# Templates

Copy these shapes. Keep every file short enough to read in a minute.

## profile.md

```markdown
# <Name>: learner profile
Updated: <date>

## Goal
<One sentence: what they want to be able to do.>

## Work
<What they do; the decisions and documents they are responsible for.>

## Out of depth
- <A recent moment, in their words, with the date they said it.>

## Already solid (skip)
- <Topic>

## Coming up
- <Date>: <event or deadline>

## Fit
- Episodes: <minutes>, <which days>
- Cards: <new per evening>; WonkoLingo account: <yes/no>; course id `<id>`
- Listening: <how episodes reach them: podcast feed, MP3s, or read-aloud of the script>
- Likes / dislikes: <voice, pace, style notes they have given>

## Unconfirmed guesses
- <Anything written here from notes that the learner has not said themselves.>
```

## outline.md

```markdown
# Outline
Updated: <date>

| Week of | Theme | Cards | Why now |
|---|---|---|---|
| <date> | <theme> | own course / a course already in WonkoLingo | <the gap or event it serves> |
| ... three more rows, looser as they go |

## Waiting list
- <Topic they asked for, or a gap from a check-in, not yet scheduled>
```

## weeks/<week>/plan.md

```markdown
# Week <n>: <theme>
Status: proposed <date> / approved <date>
Cards: own course, unit "<title>" / WonkoLingo's own course "<name>", lessons <titles>

## D1: <title>
- Idea: <one sentence>
- Story: <the real case that carries it>
- Cards ask: <five things, one line each>

## D2 ... D5

## D6: review (made after D5's cards)
```

## dN-<slug>.facts.md

```markdown
# <Episode title>: fact sheet
Checked: <date>, <how: e.g. "separate AI pass against the linked sources; no lawyer has reviewed this">

1. <Fact, one sentence.> [source](<link>)
2. <Contested view>, according to <who>. [source](<link>)

## Dropped
- <Claim that could not be confirmed, and why.>
```

## log.md entry

```markdown
## <date>: check-in, week <n>
- Results: <days played, cards missed most, removals, or "not available">
- They said: "<their words>"
- Changed: <what moved in the profile or outline>
```
